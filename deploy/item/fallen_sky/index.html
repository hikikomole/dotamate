Fallen Sky

Transform into a meteor that strikes down at the target area after 1 seconds in a 315 AoE, stunning enemies for 1.6 seconds and dealing impact damage. Continues to deal damage every 1 seconds to enemy units and buildings for 6 seconds. Building Impact Damage: 75 Building Over Time Damage: 60 Non-Building Impact Damage: 150 Non-Building Over Time Damage: 60 Fallen Sky cannot be used for 3 seconds after taking damage from an enemy hero or Roshan.
