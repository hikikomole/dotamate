ABILITIES

Способности

Transmogrify

Toggle to switch the contents of the Satchel between a pound of Feathers or Rocks. Feathers: increase movement speed by 12 and distance of forced movement effects applied to the wearer by 30%. Rocks: increase armor by 3 and decrease the distance of forced movement effects applied to the wearer by 30%.
